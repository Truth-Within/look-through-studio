# Mandatory YouTube Shorts Production Specification

Every successful job MUST produce one full-length main video and at least four separate YouTube Shorts video files. Screenshots are NOT deliverables in place of Shorts.

## Editorial selection
Analyze the source transcript and cleaned audio for at least four distinct, engaging standalone segments, preferably 20–60 seconds each by default (configurable). Favor powerful insights, surprising ideas, clear explanations, and strong emotional beats. Never invent audio, misrepresent speakers, or splice quotes into misleading statements. Preserve a timestamp provenance map. If four suitable independent segments do not exist, report the limitation and request additional material rather than fabricate.

## Independent direction
For EACH Short: create a title/hook, complete arc (hook, development, payoff), timed transcript, separate shot plan, animation timeline, and vertical-first 9:16 framing. Do not merely crop a main-video excerpt. Generate/reframe expressive illustrated characters, backgrounds, metaphors, transitions, camera moves, and readable subtitles with mobile safe zones. Maintain channel identity and continuity with the main production.

## Technical export
Export shorts/short_01.mp4 ... shorts/short_04.mp4 at minimum, default 1080x1920 H.264/AAC, with separate subtitles (SRT/VTT), thumbnails if useful, titles/descriptions/hashtags, and per-Short QC reports. Check file integrity, duration, aspect ratio, sound, voice intelligibility, caption timing, and no black/blank frames. Allow user approval before publishing.

## Publishing
Support optional YouTube upload of the main video and selected Shorts through OAuth, with individual privacy settings, scheduling where supported, and explicit approval. Do not promise platform classification or performance.
