# VISUAL DIRECTION — THE LOOK THROUGH

Reference is supplied separately as `reference_visual_style.mp4` and/or `reference_contact_sheet.jpg`. Use reference for visual language only; ignore its soundtrack, dialogue and story. Do not trace or duplicate its exact scenes, proprietary character designs, or protected frames.

Default visual system: bright paper-like white or off-white backgrounds; black ink/pencil line art; expressive simplified stick-figure humans; strong silhouettes; clear gesture acting and facial expression; sketch landscapes, clouds, hills, trees, props and symbolic objects; restrained yellow, amber, orange, occasional red accents; clear negative space; readable compositions.

Cinematic enhancement: deliberate framing, establishing/medium/close shots, foreground/background depth, parallax, smooth motivated camera motion, hand-drawn transitions, thoughtful rhythm, controlled color accents, coherent recurring character designs, animated metaphors, motion timing that follows narration meaning, restrained atmospheric lighting and depth.

Adaptive styles: schematic diagrams, historical ink illustrations, abstract conceptual imagery, scientific chalkboard/technical drawing, architectural sketches, maps and infographics when appropriate. Maintain the same typography, motion vocabulary, visual hierarchy and restrained palette. Avoid abrupt inconsistent styles without a narrative reason.

Scene planning rules: each spoken idea gets an intentional visual beat; do not just illustrate nouns. Define what audience should understand/feel, how visual evolves, why camera moves, and how scene transitions. Avoid repeated stock assets, AI morphing artifacts, illegible on-screen text, inconsistent anatomy, and visually irrelevant filler.

Four cinematic Shorts: choose at least four distinct, meaningful moments from the original source and produce separately directed, fully animated 9:16 vertical short videos. Reuse assets where suitable, but reframe or regenerate for portrait composition. Each needs a hook, coherent development, strong payoff, expressive scenes, clear captions, and appropriate sound. Stills and screenshots do not count toward this requirement.
